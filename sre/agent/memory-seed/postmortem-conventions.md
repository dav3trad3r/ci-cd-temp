# Post-mortem conventions

Post-mortems are blameless: describe what happened and why, never who to blame. Use these sections, in this order:

1. **Summary**: two sentences.
2. **Impact**: who was affected, for how long, and how badly.
3. **Timeline**: times in Brisbane time (AEST).
4. **Cause**: the technical cause, and why our checks didn't catch it.
5. **Fix**: what changed, with the pull request link.
6. **Follow-ups**: one to three actions that stop it happening again, each with an owner role (tool owner, platform lead).

End with one line on the impact for staff and customers.
